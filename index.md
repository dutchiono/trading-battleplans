## 🟢 Long Setup Ideas

### Pre-Market Gapper Longs

**Criteria:**
- Gap up 3-8% on catalyst (earnings, news, upgrade)
- Strong pre-market volume (3x average)
- Holding near highs in pre-market
- Entry: Consolidation break above pre-market high
- Stop: Below pre-market low
- Target: Extension move 5-10% above entry

**Today's Candidates:**
- **KO:** Already +4%, watch for dip-buy if pulls back to opening range
- **DD:** +6.9% momentum, continuation possible if holds gains

### Breakout Longs

**Setup:**
- Multi-day consolidation (3-5 days)
- Tight range near resistance
- Volume dries up during consolidation
- Entry: Break above consolidation high with volume surge
- Stop: Below consolidation low
- Target: Measured move (consolidation range added to breakout point)

### Oversold Bounce Longs

**Criteria:**
- Down 10%+ in recent days without news
- RSI under 30
- Near support level (moving average, prior low)
- Entry: First green candle with volume after selling exhaustion
- Stop: Below recent low
- Target: 3-5% bounce to resistance
